# 🎬 CINECHAOS - CHAOS BY DESIGN (CSI VIT)

> **Event**: CSI VIT - *Chaos By Design*  
> **Problem Statement 3**: Cinema seat selection  
> **Test Case**: Select seat **F12** from an interactive seat map grid and confirm selection.  
> **Winning Strategy**: Maximum frustration, psychological warfare, hilarious UX anti-patterns — **AND IT STILL 100% WORKS!**

## 😈 Chaos Mechanics (Show this to Judges!)

1. **Evasive Seat F12 (Hover Dodge)**:
   - When hovering near seat **F12**, it physically flees from the cursor 3 times with bouncy physics and whoosh sound effects.
2. **Decoy Rejection & Misclick Tax**:
   - Clicking any other seat rejects the selection with a buzzer sound and increases the live **Rage Index** and **Misclicks** counter.
3. **The Bot Hold Trap**:
   - On the first click of F12, CineChaos announces: *"User 'CinemaGeek88' in Mumbai is currently holding seat F12!"* with a tense 3-second lock release countdown.
4. **The Mandatory Stale Popcorn Upsell**:
   - Once unlocked, a popup demands a ₹1,899 stale popcorn combo.
   - The close `[✕]` button and `"No thanks"` button dart away from the mouse cursor when hovered.
5. **Ocular 3D Headrest Calibration**:
   - Because of IMAX curved geometry, the user must calibrate the headrest slider to exactly **12.0°**.
6. **The Fleeing "Confirm Selection" Button**:
   - Once F12 is secured, the final Confirm button dashes away 3 times before panting: *"🥵 Fine, click me!"*.
7. **Triply-Negated Final Confirmation**:
   - A modal tests their reading comprehension with deceptive cancelation options, where the golden **"CONFIRM SEAT F12 NOW"** completes the challenge.
8. **Test Case Victory & Confetti**:
   - Upon confirmation, the live timer stops, victory fanfare plays, confetti showers the screen, and an authentic CSI VIT cinema ticket stub is generated!

1. **Find Row F, Seat 12**:
   - Hover near F12 three times until it stops dodging.

   - Move cursor to the bottom right button 3 times until it exhausts, then click it.
6. **Final Modal**:
   - Click the big gold button: **"CONFIRM SEAT F12 NOW"**.
